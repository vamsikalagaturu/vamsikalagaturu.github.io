01 Research

Reusable building blocks for physical intelligence.

I design software and control abstractions that let robots act deliberately in the real world — where geometry, contact, and uncertainty all matter at once.

Contact-rich manipulation

Controllers and skills for tasks where the robot must push, slide, and stay in contact — reasoning about forces, not just positions.

Composable robot models

Models and constraints you can snap together and reuse across tasks and platforms — reducing the glue code behind every skill.

Constraint-based control

Expressing behaviour as tasks and constraints, then solving them online so mobile manipulators move smoothly and safely.

Perception & viewpoint planning

Deciding where to look — planning viewpoints to map unknown, confined spaces so manipulation has the information it needs.

04 About

A bit about me.

I’m a robotics researcher focused on the software and control that make manipulation dependable. After an M.Sc. in Autonomous Systems at Bonn-Rhein-Sieg University, I joined the SECORO group at the University of Bremen, where I work on composable models for contact-rich mobile manipulation.

I care about robotics that is reusable and honest about the physical world — abstractions that hold up when the robot actually touches something. Away from the lab, I like clean interfaces, good tooling, and the occasional web experiment like this one.
